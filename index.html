About Me

I'm a SCADA Technician and Production Foreman based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia, with over 4 years of hands-on experience in industrial automation and production operations at Saveto Group.

My core work involves operating and programming Siemens S7-1200/1500 PLC systems, monitoring WinCC SCADA in real-time, managing Fuji Ace robotic systems, and leading production teams across demanding 12-hour shifts.

Before automation, I built a strong foundation in aerospace-grade quality assurance at O/E/N India Ltd. — testing electromechanical components to MIL standards — and in PCB stencil design at SFO Technologies using Altium Designer.

Beyond the factory floor, I build DIY electronics, explore workflow automation with n8n, and enjoy applying engineering thinking to personal projects.
